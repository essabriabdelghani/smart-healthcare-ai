# Back Pain

Back pain is one of the most common medical complaints, most often caused
by muscle or ligament strain, poor posture, or age-related changes to the
spine. It is usually not dangerous and improves within a few weeks with
conservative management (rest, movement, analgesics).

Back pain accompanied by loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness in the
groin area, progressive leg weakness, unexplained weight loss, fever, or
occurring after significant trauma requires urgent evaluation, as these
may indicate a serious spinal condition.
