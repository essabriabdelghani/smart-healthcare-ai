# Fever

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually above 38°C
(100.4°F), often caused by an infection. It is part of the immune
response and is not itself a disease but a symptom. Common causes include
viral infections (colds, flu), bacterial infections, and, less commonly,
inflammatory conditions.

Most fevers in otherwise healthy adults resolve on their own with rest and
fluids. Evaluation is warranted when fever is very high (above 39.5°C),
lasts more than three days, or is accompanied by other concerning symptoms
such as difficulty breathing, severe pain, confusion, or a persistent
rash.
