# Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs,
which may fill with fluid or pus. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses,
or fungi. Symptoms include cough (often with phlegm), fever, chills,
difficulty breathing, and chest pain that worsens with breathing or
coughing.

Pneumonia can range from mild to life-threatening, and is particularly
serious in infants, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems
or chronic conditions. Difficulty breathing, confusion, low oxygen
saturation, or bluish lips in someone with suspected pneumonia are signs
requiring urgent care.
