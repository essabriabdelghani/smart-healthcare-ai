# Vomiting

Vomiting is the forceful expulsion of stomach contents through the mouth.
It can result from gastrointestinal infections, food poisoning, motion
sickness, migraine, pregnancy, medication side effects, or, less commonly,
bowel obstruction or increased pressure inside the skull.

Vomiting blood (which may look like coffee grounds), persistent vomiting
preventing fluid intake, vomiting with severe abdominal pain, or vomiting
following a head injury are warning signs requiring urgent care. Isolated,
short-lived vomiting from a stomach bug is typically self-limiting.
