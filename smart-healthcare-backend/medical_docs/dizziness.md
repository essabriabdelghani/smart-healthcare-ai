# Dizziness

Dizziness is a broad term describing sensations such as lightheadedness,
unsteadiness, or a spinning feeling (vertigo). Causes include inner ear
disorders, low blood pressure, dehydration, anemia, medication side
effects, and anxiety.

Dizziness accompanied by chest pain, severe headache, difficulty speaking,
weakness on one side of the body, or fainting requires urgent evaluation,
as it may point to a cardiac or neurological cause. Brief, situational
dizziness (e.g., standing up quickly) without other symptoms is usually
benign.
