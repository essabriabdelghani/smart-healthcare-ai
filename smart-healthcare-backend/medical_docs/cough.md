# Cough

Cough is a reflex that clears the airways of irritants, mucus, or foreign
material. Acute cough (less than 3 weeks) is most often due to a viral
respiratory infection. Chronic cough (more than 8 weeks) can be caused by
asthma, acid reflux, postnasal drip, or, less commonly, chronic
respiratory disease.

A cough with high fever, difficulty breathing, coughing up blood, chest
pain, or significant unintentional weight loss warrants medical
evaluation. A mild, dry cough associated with a common cold usually
resolves within one to two weeks.
