# Confusion

Confusion refers to an inability to think clearly, maintain attention, or
recognize one's surroundings normally. Acute confusion (delirium) can be
caused by infections, low blood sugar, low oxygen levels, medication
effects, alcohol or drug intoxication/withdrawal, head injury, or stroke.

Sudden-onset confusion, especially combined with fever, severe headache,
weakness on one side of the body, or slurred speech, is a medical
emergency and may indicate stroke, meningitis, or sepsis. Gradual,
long-standing confusion in older adults may instead reflect a chronic
cognitive condition and warrants a different diagnostic approach.
