# Diarrhea

Diarrhea is the passage of loose or watery stools more frequently than
normal. Acute diarrhea is most often caused by viral or bacterial
gastrointestinal infections, or food intolerance. Chronic diarrhea
(lasting more than 4 weeks) can indicate conditions such as inflammatory
bowel disease or malabsorption.

Diarrhea with signs of dehydration (reduced urination, dizziness, dry
mouth), high fever, blood in the stool, or severe abdominal pain requires
medical attention. Mild, short-lived diarrhea is typically managed with
fluid replacement and rest.
