# Abdominal Pain

Abdominal pain is discomfort felt anywhere between the chest and the
groin. Causes range from benign and self-limiting (gas, indigestion,
constipation, muscle strain) to conditions requiring prompt treatment
(appendicitis, gallstones, bowel obstruction, ectopic pregnancy,
pancreatitis).

Severe, sudden-onset abdominal pain, pain accompanied by fever, vomiting
blood, inability to pass stool or gas, abdominal rigidity, or pain that
progressively worsens and localizes (e.g., migrating to the lower right
quadrant, suggestive of appendicitis) requires urgent evaluation. Mild,
intermittent abdominal discomfort without alarming features is more often
benign.
