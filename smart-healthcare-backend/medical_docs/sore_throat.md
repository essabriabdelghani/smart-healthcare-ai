# Sore Throat

Sore throat is pain, scratchiness, or irritation of the throat, most
commonly caused by viral infections (common cold, flu) and, less often, by
bacterial infection (streptococcal pharyngitis), allergies, or dry air.

A sore throat with high fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing,
drooling, or a muffled voice may indicate a more serious infection and
warrants prompt evaluation. Most viral sore throats resolve within a
week with supportive care (fluids, rest, throat lozenges).
