# Difficulty Breathing

Difficulty breathing describes labored or effortful respiration, often
overlapping with shortness of breath but emphasizing the mechanical effort
of breathing rather than the subjective sensation of air hunger. Causes
include airway obstruction, asthma exacerbation, pneumonia, anaphylaxis,
and heart failure.

Signs of severe respiratory distress include use of accessory neck and
chest muscles to breathe, inability to speak full sentences, wheezing or
stridor, and low oxygen saturation (below 94%). These signs require
immediate medical attention.
