# Nausea

Nausea is the sensation of an urge to vomit. It commonly accompanies
gastrointestinal infections, motion sickness, pregnancy, migraine, and
medication side effects. It can also be a symptom of more serious
conditions when combined with other signs.

Nausea accompanied by severe abdominal pain, chest pain, signs of
dehydration, or persisting for more than 24-48 hours without improvement
should prompt medical evaluation. Mild, transient nausea related to a
known trigger (e.g., motion sickness) is usually not concerning.
