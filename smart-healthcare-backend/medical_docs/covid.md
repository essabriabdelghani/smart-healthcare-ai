# COVID-19

COVID-19 is a respiratory illness caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Common
symptoms include fever, cough, fatigue, loss of taste or smell, sore
throat, and shortness of breath. Severity ranges from asymptomatic or mild
illness to severe pneumonia and respiratory failure, particularly in
older adults or those with underlying health conditions.

Warning signs requiring urgent care include difficulty breathing,
persistent chest pain or pressure, confusion, and low oxygen saturation.
Mild cases are typically managed with rest, fluids, and symptom
monitoring at home, with isolation to reduce transmission.
