# Severe Headache

A severe headache is intense head pain that significantly interferes with
daily activity. Common causes include migraine, tension-type headache,
cluster headache, and sinus infection. Less commonly, severe headache can
signal a more serious condition.

"Red flag" features that warrant urgent evaluation include: the worst
headache of one's life with sudden onset ("thunderclap" headache), headache
with fever and neck stiffness, headache after head trauma, headache with
neurological symptoms (weakness, vision loss, confusion, slurred speech),
and new headache patterns in people over 50. These combinations may
indicate conditions such as subarachnoid hemorrhage, meningitis, or
stroke.
