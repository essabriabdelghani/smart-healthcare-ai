# Bleeding

Bleeding (hemorrhage) is the loss of blood from the circulatory system,
either externally (visible) or internally (not visible, but suspected from
symptoms such as dizziness, pale skin, rapid heartbeat, or abdominal
swelling). Causes range from minor cuts to trauma, gastrointestinal
ulcers, ruptured blood vessels, and bleeding disorders.

Severe or uncontrolled bleeding, bleeding accompanied by dizziness or
fainting, vomiting blood, or passing black/bloody stools are medical
emergencies requiring immediate care. Minor, controlled bleeding from
superficial wounds is generally manageable with basic first aid.
