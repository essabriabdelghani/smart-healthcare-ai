# Shortness of Breath (Dyspnea)

Shortness of breath, or dyspnea, is the sensation of not being able to
breathe well enough. It can be caused by lung conditions (asthma, COPD,
pneumonia, pulmonary embolism), heart conditions (heart failure, heart
attack), anemia, anxiety, or physical deconditioning.

Sudden, severe shortness of breath — especially with chest pain, bluish
lips or fingertips, confusion, or an inability to speak in full sentences
— is a medical emergency. Gradual dyspnea that worsens with exertion may
indicate a chronic cardiac or respiratory condition and warrants clinical
evaluation, particularly when combined with low oxygen saturation.
