# Chest Pain

Chest pain is discomfort or pain felt anywhere in the chest area, ranging
from a sharp stab to a dull ache. It can originate from the heart, lungs,
esophagus, muscles, ribs, or nerves. Cardiac causes include angina and
myocardial infarction (heart attack); non-cardiac causes include acid
reflux, muscle strain, anxiety, and costochondritis (inflammation of the
rib cartilage).

Red flags requiring urgent evaluation: pain that is crushing or pressure-like,
radiates to the arm, jaw, or back, is accompanied by shortness of breath,
sweating, nausea, or dizziness, or lasts more than a few minutes. Chest
pain combined with these signs should prompt immediate emergency care, as
it may indicate a heart attack.

Non-urgent chest pain is often sharp, localized, worsens with movement or
palpation, and is not accompanied by systemic symptoms.
