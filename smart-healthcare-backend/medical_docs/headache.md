# Headache

Headache is pain felt anywhere in the head or upper neck. The vast
majority are primary headaches — tension-type headache or migraine — with
no underlying structural cause. Secondary headaches result from another
condition (sinus infection, dehydration, medication overuse, or, rarely,
a more serious neurological cause).

Most headaches are manageable with rest, hydration, and over-the-counter
analgesics. See the "Severe Headache" reference for warning signs that
distinguish a routine headache from one requiring urgent evaluation.
